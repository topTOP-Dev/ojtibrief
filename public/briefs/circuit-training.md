# Circuit Training Briefing
## Traffic Pattern — VTCC RWY 36 Right

**Session Type:** OJTI Circuit Briefing
**Airport:** VTCC / Chiang Mai International
**Pattern:** Right-hand, RWY 36

---

# Traffic Pattern Overview

The training aircraft (TRN002) will fly a **right-hand traffic pattern** for RWY 36.

- **Pattern altitude:** 1,000 ft AAL (approx. 1,500 ft AMSL at VTCC)
- **Circuit speed:** ~90 kt
- **Each leg duration:** 1 minute (60 seconds)
- **Total circuit time:** 5 minutes

---

# Circuit Legs

| Leg        | Heading   | Duration | Description              |
|------------|-----------|----------|--------------------------|
| Downwind   | 180° (S)  | 1:00     | Parallel, opposite runway |
| Base       | 270° (W)  | 1:00     | Turning toward runway     |
| Final      | 360° (N)  | 1:00     | On centreline, landing    |
| Upwind     | 360° (N)  | 1:00     | Climbing after touch-and-go |
| Crosswind  | 090° (E)  | 1:00     | Turning to join downwind  |

---

# Key Correlation Trigger

**TRN002 leaves downwind when TRN001 (inbound) reaches 15 NM.**

This is the starting point for all correlation planning.

***

At that exact moment:
- **TRN001:** 15 NM inbound, T-3:00 to touchdown
- **TRN002:** turning base leg

# Student Objectives

1. Maintain situational awareness of **both** aircraft simultaneously
2. Identify when TRN002 is safe to be cleared touch-and-go
3. Confirm TRN002 is airborne and clear before TRN001 crosses the threshold
4. Communicate clearly with both TRN001 and TRN002

---

# Separation Requirement

TRN002 must be **airborne and clear of the runway** before TRN001 crosses the threshold.

- **Target:** TRN002 on upwind when TRN001 is at 5 NM
- **Do not** clear TRN001 for approach if TRN002 has not reported touch-and-go
- Monitor TRN002 position on every leg

***

Separation is **the student's responsibility** to verbalise and confirm.

---

# Communication Flow

**TRN002 — Downwind call:**
> "VTCC Tower, TRN002, downwind runway 36, touch-and-go"

**Tower response (when inbound is at ~15 NM):**
> "TRN002, number one, cleared touch-and-go runway 36, wind 010/05"

**TRN001 — Approach:**
> "TRN001, continue approach, traffic is a light aircraft on short final, report visual"

**TRN001 — After TRN002 clears:**
> "TRN001, cleared ILS approach runway 36, no delay, contact Tower 118.1"

---

# Common Errors to Watch For

- Clearing TRN001 before TRN002 reports clear of the runway
- Losing track of TRN002's position during final/upwind transition
- Delayed clearance to TRN002 on downwind causing go-around
- Forgetting to pass traffic information to TRN001

---

# Debrief Checklist

- [ ] Correct timing of TRN002 clearance on downwind
- [ ] Separation maintained throughout
- [ ] Traffic information passed to TRN001
- [ ] TRN002 confirmed airborne before TRN001 threshold crossing
- [ ] Situational awareness verbalised clearly
