# Control of Arriving Aircraft

## Transfer of communication

### APP shall transfer communication to TWR:

1. **IFR** at LASOX.
2. **VFR** at 10 NM, or the point that the pilot can visual the aerodrome (Aerodrome insight) which is not further than 10 NM.

## Information provided to arrival

1. (Instructions to circuit), Runway to be used
2. Surface wind direction and speed, including significant variations therefrom.
3. QNH

## Example phraseologies:

- ** Join Right Downwind Runway 36 ** , Wind 2-8-0 degrees 5 khots, QNH 1-0-0-5. Expect Number 2 after traffic A320 20 NM.
- ** Join 5 NM Initial ** , Wind 2-8-0 degrees 5 khots, QNH 1-0-0-5. Report 5 NM Initial.
- ** Join Helicopter Route at San Klang** , 1 600 feet, Report over San Klang.
- After San Klang, **Join Right Downwind Runway 36 (or Join 1 mile east of aerodrome)**, Standby for crossing runway.

# Separation

## Separation of landing aircraft and preceding landing aircraft and departing aircraft using the same runway.

### a landing aircraft will not normally be permitted to cross the runway threshold on its final approach **until:**

1. The preceding departing aircraft **has crossed the end** of the runway-in-use, or
2. **has started a turn**, or
3. until all preceding landing aircraft are **clear of the runway-in-use**.

# Clearance to land

## An aircraft may be cleared to land when there is reasonable assurance that the separation will exist when the aircraft crosses ** the runway threshold **

## Clearance to land shall not be issued before:

1. the aircraft has established final approach course of an in instrument approach; or
2. the aircraft has been sighted by the tower controller:

- on downwind leg of the circuit pattern; or
- on base leg; or
- on final

Observation by radar satisfies th sighting requirement.

## When the landing area is occupied by another aircraft or is obstructed, arriving aircraft may be issued with a clearance to:

1. **continue approach** if there is no immediate assurance that the landing areas will become available. This shall be followed by the appropriate clearance; or
2. **go around, or orbit** if in a position to do so, should the landing area not be available. When required, a clearance to commence a second approach or hold shall follow these instructions. The nature of the obstruction shall be advised if not apparent to the approaching aircraft.

## Missed Approach Procedures

- [MATS II, 6.2] TWR shall ensure that the missed approach aircraft is separated from
  all flights operating in aerodrome traffic circuit. Once upon local flights are being carried out, the phraseology described below should be given to the aircraft which suspects to make or make missed approach: ** CONTINUE RUNWAY HEADING TO 4 000 FEET **
