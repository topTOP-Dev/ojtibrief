# ATC Radar Briefing
## Inbound Sequence — VTCC Chiang Mai

**Session Type:** OJTI Radar Briefing
**Airport:** VTCC / Chiang Mai International
**Runway in Use:** RWY 36 (ILS Approach)

---

# Scenario Overview

A single inbound aircraft is approaching VTCC from the south along the 360° radial.

- **Callsign:** TRN001
- **Route:** Straight-in, extended centreline RWY 36
- **Initial contact:** 80 NM south of VTCC
- **Approach type:** ILS RWY 36

---

# Radar Scope Setup

- **Display range:** 100 NM
- **Range rings:** 80 · 60 · 40 · 25 · 20 · 10 · 7 · 5 NM
- **Inbound track:** 360° (south to north, direct to VTCC)

The aircraft flies a straight-in approach — no vectoring is required for this exercise.

# Student Objectives

1. Identify TRN001 at first call (80 NM)
2. Issue descent clearances at appropriate ranges
3. Monitor separation with circuit training traffic (TRN002)
4. Sequence the approach and maintain situational awareness
5. Hand off to Tower frequency at **7 NM**

---

# Descent Profile Guidance

**80 NM — Initial Contact**
> Expect aircraft at FL270. Descend to FL080.

**40 NM — Mid-Route**
> Descend to 5,000 ft, reduce speed 250 kt.

**20 NM — Approach Phase**
> Descend to 3,000 ft. Expect ILS capture.

**10 NM — Final Approach**
> Cleared ILS approach RWY 36. Contact Tower 118.1.

---

# Separation with Circuit Traffic

**TRN002** is conducting circuit training at VTCC.

- TRN002 leaves downwind when TRN001 reaches **15 NM**
- TRN001 continues inbound on ILS centreline
- TRN002 should be clear of the runway before TRN001 crosses the threshold

***

**Cross-check:** When TRN001 is at 5 NM, TRN002 should be on upwind.

# Key Phraseologies

> "TRN001, VTCC Approach, radar contact, [XX] miles south of VTCC, [altitude], QNH [xxx]"

**Descent clearance:**
> "TRN001, descend and maintain [altitude], QNH [xxx]"

**Approach clearance:**
> "TRN001, cleared ILS approach runway 36, report established"

**Frequency change:**
> "TRN001, contact VTCC Tower 118.1, goodbye"
